## 1. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

When reviewing or building landing pages, checkout flows, or signups:

### Hero Section Formula

1. **Clear Value Proposition**: One sentence stating the exact outcome for the specific audience.
2. **Clarifying Subtitle**: Explains how it works, eliminating the #1 objection.
3. **Primary CTA**: High-intent, action-oriented verb (e.g., "Iniciar prueba de 14 días", "Solicitar demo").
4. **Secondary CTA**: Low-friction alternative (e.g., "Ver cómo funciona", "Explorar catálogo").
5. **Social Proof Anchor**: Trust badges, client counts, or ISO/compliance certifications immediately visible above the fold.

### Form & Signup Friction Reduction

- Strip all non-essential fields (ask only email + password upfront).
- Deferred gratification: Show value before requiring credit card.
- Inline validation with immediate positive reinforcement.

## 2. High-Conversion Copywriting Frameworks

Never write generic marketing copy. Always select and apply a formal persuasion framework:

### A. PAS (Problem - Agitation - Solution)

- **Problem**: Pinpoint the painful reality the user faces (e.g., "Perder ventas por desincronización de inventario entre sucursales").
- **Agitation**: Quantify the emotional and financial cost of leaving it unsolved ("Horas cuadrando Excel, clientes frustrados y pedidos cancelados").
- **Solution**: Present the product as the inevitable, seamless resolution ("Cendaro ERP sincroniza tus ventas y stock en tiempo real en una sola pantalla").

### B. AIDA (Attention - Interest - Desire - Action)

- Ideal for launch emails, product announcements, and cold outreach.

### C. BAB (Before - After - Bridge)

- **Before**: The chaotic current state.
- **After**: The calm, organized, automated future.
- **Bridge**: How the product bridges the gap.

### D. Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)

- Focus entirely on the progress the customer wants to make, not on technical feature lists.
- Formula: _"When I [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome]."_

## 3. Technical SEO & AI Search Optimization (GEO)

1. **Title & Meta Description**:
   - Title: `[Primary Keyword] — [Brand USP] | [Brand Name]` (Under 60 characters).
   - Description: Actionable summary containing secondary keyword and clear CTA (Under 155 characters).
2. **Structured Data (Schema.org JSON-LD)**:
   - Implement `SoftwareApplication`, `Organization`, and `FAQPage` schema on public pages.
3. **Semantic Hierarchy**:
   - Exactly one `<h1>` per page.
   - Strict `<h2>` and `<h3>` nested order without skips.
4. **Answer Engine Optimization (Perplexity / ChatGPT)**:
   - Provide concise, 40-word definitive answer paragraphs directly under major H2 headings.

## 4. SaaS Pricing & Packaging Strategy

- **Dual-Currency Clarity**: In mixed-currency markets (USD / Bs), clearly state billing mechanics and exchange rate anchors.
- **Three-Tier Architecture**:
  - _Starter_: Low barrier to entry, self-serve, single store.
  - _Professional (Recommended Anchor)_: Omnichannel, multi-warehouse, automations.
  - _Enterprise_: Custom integrations, SLA, dedicated account manager.
- **Value Metric**: Price based on business scale (e.g., transactions/month or users), not arbitrary feature gating.
